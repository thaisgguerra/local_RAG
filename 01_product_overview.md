# Product overview: Parcel Locker (fictitious company "Northwind Lockers")

Note: this knowledge base is fictitious. It exists only to demonstrate and test the RAG pipeline.

## What the product does
Northwind Lockers makes smart parcel lockers for apartment buildings and offices. Couriers drop parcels into a locker and residents collect them with a QR code sent by SMS or the mobile app.
Each locker bank has between 12 and 48 compartments in three sizes: small, medium and large.

## User journey
1. The courier scans the parcel barcode at the locker screen and the system assigns a compartment.
2. The resident receives a notification with a pickup QR code, valid for 72 hours.
3. The resident scans the code at the locker and the compartment opens.
4. If the parcel is not collected in 72 hours, the building manager is notified.

## Product limits (do not claim)
- The locker does not have refrigerated compartments.
- The locker does not accept cash on delivery.
- The locker does not weigh parcels.
