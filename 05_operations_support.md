# Operations and support: Parcel Locker

## Support channels
Support is available by e-mail and by chat inside the mobile app, Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00.
Phone support is offered only for enterprise customers.

## Service levels
Critical issues, such as a locker bank that is completely offline, have a 4-hour first response target.
Normal issues have a 1-business-day first response target.

## Field visits
A technician visit is scheduled when remote reset does not fix the problem. The average visit happens within 3 business days.
Technicians carry spare locks, sensors and a spare screen.

## Customer onboarding
Installation takes one day per locker bank. After installation, the building manager receives a 30-minute training session.

## Invoicing
Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month. Customers can request a corrected invoice until the 10th of the same month. [TO VERIFY] The deadline may move to the 15th.
